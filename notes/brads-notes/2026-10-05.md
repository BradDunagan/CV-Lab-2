## Calibrating from what was read

reference is its own measured value at the reference pose (step 0 of x, y, z

## The soft shadow edge

Brad: go ahead with the soft shadow edge. Branch `shadow-edge` (pushed, not
merged): fitBand soft edges, free or anchored to another edge; scripts in
2026-10-05-shadow-edge/ on that branch.

- Free soft edge: ledge frame 60/20 -2 mm, gain 1.88, but the base edge
  went from +0.49 to -0.18. The ramp in the profile starts AT the crease;
  a flat ledge level then a soft step can't make it.
- Anchored (ramp starts at the edge): ledge frames 0.31-0.41 -> 0.05-0.29,
  base edge now pushed OUT up to 0.29. Clean frames gain 1.00-1.02 and stay.
  Can't place an edge under a penumbra better than ~0.2 px.
- The base doesn't move. Its line from the clean +2 mm frame of the view,
  held, two-edge fit otherwise: ledge frames 0.09-0.15, clean within 0.03.
- Held + anchored ramp: erratic (0.03 / 0.41), runs away on clean frames.

So: soft edge = ledge detector (gain 1.4-2.5 vs 1.0-1.1). Fix = hold the
still edge from a frame without a ledge. 50-degree views had no clean pair
at +2 mm to hold, which a real version must handle.
