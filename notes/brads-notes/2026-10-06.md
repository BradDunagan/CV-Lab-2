## 1. An aperture that is not a box

`fitBand` takes `profile: 'smooth'`: the projected unit pixel (trapezoid)
convolved with three equal boxes -- a quadratic B-spline, Gaussian-like and
still piecewise polynomial (inclusion-exclusion over the boxes, lower half
only, terms cached). The aperture keeps its meaning, the width of the box
with the same variance: s = sqrt((w^2 - 1)/3); w <= 1 is the box.

Checked: two boxes give `coverage` to 1e-14; density matches the numeric
derivative to 4e-10; mass 1 and variance exact. Box path unchanged
(x sweep and the whole hinged solve identical to the 10-05 numbers).

Scripts in 2026-10-06-aperture/: profile.sh (a variant re-analysed under a
profile, beside the box results, via symlinked generated/ names), summary.js
(test-pose RMS: ten multi-view sets as §5's tables, and four views),
diag.js / linear.js / held.js (refit error per pair, its non-linear part,
and the fit at a held aperture), lone.js, wide.js, synthetic.js.

Synthetic strip (area-sampled at 32x, Gaussian 0.8 px on the grid):

    smooth fits the lone edge 3x better (rms 2.8e-3 vs 8.3e-3), aperture 3.00;
    gap error at the measured aperture: smooth -0.001 / +0.016 / -0.085,
    box -0.100 / +0.077 / +0.035  (2.5 px, 4 px, 1.5 px at 30 deg)

So the shape is right. On the real blurred renders, with the aperture
measured as before (median of lone segments), it is WORSE: x-blur front
pair gap error rms 0.092 -> 0.240, non-linear part 0.031 -> 0.063; y-blur
loses 5 of 16 readings. The lone segments read 3.23 under smooth (2.91 under
box); the truth is nearer 3.0. Real lone edges have tails a step does not
(shading, rounding), and the smooth model turns tails into width.

Held apertures, x-blur front pair (rms / non-linear, px):

    box at its measured (2.82-2.98)   0.092 / 0.031
    smooth 2.8                        0.175 / 0.016

    smooth 3.1                        0.218 / 0.026

Shape right, measurement wrong, and steep: 0.1 px of aperture is ~0.1 px of
gap. Measured on the pair itself where it is wide (wide.js), the smooth
aperture is 2.95-3.05 on every front-pair frame of x-blur, and the gap error
0.00-0.02 (box: 2.7-2.8, +0.03-0.06).

Tried: one aperture per image from all its wide pairs -- worse everywhere
(clean box front pair 0.029 -> 0.074): shadow-line pairs join in and it
jumps frame to frame. Then each pair its own where wide, gated on the
DETECTED gap -- worse: true 1.36 px gaps detected wider fit their own
aperture and it trades with the gap. Gated on the fitted gap (>= 2 px and
1.2 apertures): neutral on refit alone; what it is for is the carry, which
takes the aperture to the narrow frames. Queued: queue.sh (smooth-pairs,
box-pairs on clean, blur, distort2, all).
