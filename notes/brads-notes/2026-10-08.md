# 2026-10-08

Pre-port item 1 (the rr port page): which detected edge is which part,
without the renderer's truth. Scripts and logs in 2026-10-08-identify/;
design-lab-model.md §5, "A thirty-eighth". Branch `identify`.

## Tools

believed-truth.js: a run's shots again, Cube2 moved by a seeded error per
pose (one for all four views), rendered --truth --samples 1 at the source's
size. ~20-25 s a shot. --errors <file> gives the errors instead.

gap-sweep --identify <dir>: lab-cli gets --truth <dir>, so identification,
pairing, the measuring point, carry and tracking all use the belief. Each row
is then scored by scoredAgainst: the real facing pair nearest the predicted
T.at (within 10 px), its gap at T (0 at contact), errors shifted by
T.gap - real. endsTruePx re-read the same way (added after the runs; only the
solve's truth column used it).

## Why b15 costs

Not wrong readings: tracked readings all present, |error| against the real
lines unchanged (median 0.04-0.07 px, max 0.25). Detections lose the match
to Cube2's predicted edge (42 rows of 269, against 10) but the solve uses
tracked. Predicted vs real gap median 1.2-1.7 px (b05: 0.4-0.65). Likely the
measuring points moving with the belief; not isolated.

Per-pose (b05 vs b15 test): the same poses are worst, larger with the
belief (pose 10: belief -2.35, 0, -2.2 mm -> -0.10 x, -0.11 z).

## For the port

Prediction through pt-lab costs 20 s a frame; rr needs the parts' edges
projected from a pose in milliseconds (silhouette + creases + occlusion).
Calibration sweeps want a belief within ~0.5 mm; test poses tolerate 1.5 mm
with a second identification, which a loop does anyway.
