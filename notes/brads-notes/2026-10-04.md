# The stack, off the axes and turned

The 10-02 position solve was scored on the poses its Jacobian was measured
on, every one of them moved along a single axis. Brad's two follow-ups, done
together: poses off the axes, and the top cube turned about the vertical.

## What was built

- **`gap-sweep --poses "x,y,z[,turn];..."`.** A list of poses instead of a
  sweep: a displacement from contact in mm and a turn in degrees. One shot per
  pose per view.
- **Readings at the ends.** Each pair's gap is read at its middle and a sixth
  in from each end: `endsTruePx`, `endsDetectedPx`, the refit's `endsPx`.
- **`npm run position` with `--turn`, `--test` and `--readings`.** The turn as
  a fourth unknown, from a `--poses` run of turns at the reference; test poses
  scored separately from the poses the Jacobian came from.

```bash
npm run gap-sweep -- --name stack-2g-turn --scene saved:stack-2 --moving Cube2 --target Cube \
    --poses "0,4,0,-3;0,4,0,-1.5;0,4,0,0;0,4,0,1.5;0,4,0,3" \
    --yaw 35,60 --elevation 20,50 --script pipelines/pairs.lab
npm run gap-sweep -- --name stack-2g-test --scene saved:stack-2 --moving Cube2 --target Cube \
    --poses "1.5,5,-1,0;-2,3,2,0;0.7,6,1.3,0;-1.2,4.5,-2.5,0;2.5,2.5,0.8,0;1,4,-1,1.5;-1.5,5,1.5,-2;0.5,3,-0.5,2.5;-0.8,6,2.2,-1;2,4.5,0,-3" \
    --yaw 35,60 --elevation 20,50 --script pipelines/pairs.lab
npm run position -- --x results/stack-2g-x/pairs --y results/stack-2g-y/pairs --z results/stack-2g-z/pairs \
    --turn results/stack-2g-turn/pairs --test results/stack-2g-test/pairs --max-views 4
```

60 renders. The test poses' y is 2.5 to 6 mm (the reference is a 4 mm lift);
x and z within ±2.5 mm; five turned by 1 to 3 degrees.

**About 0.1 to 0.3 mm and 0.1 to 0.25 degrees from two views, about twice the
error of the axis-aligned poses.** The worst pose in most combinations is
pose 10, turned −3 degrees: −0.42, −0.21, −0.46 mm and +0.58 deg with all four
views.

## What the turn needed

**Its Jacobian at the middle of a pair is nearly nothing**, 0.03 to 0.11 px per
degree, and at the ends it is large and opposite: end 1 −0.31 to −0.78, end 2
+0.30 to +0.80 px/deg. Solved from middles only, with the turn as an unknown,
every pose was refused by `--max-sigma`: the turn's sigma was 14 to 45 degrees
per pixel.

**One view with the ends determines the turn and not the rest**: 0.75 to 1.6
degrees per px, and 500 to 2100 mm per px on x, y and z. Six readings, four
unknowns, and they still do not separate the three displacements.

## Worth noting

- **The refit's end readings are worse than its middles.** At the reference
  pose, front pair, the truth's ends differ by +0.21 px; the refit's by −0.13.
  A line fitted over 100 px places its middle well and its slope less well.
  The end readings still carry the turn, but this is the place to improve.
- **The same-elevation pair (35/20 + 60/20) did best on the test poses**, where
  on the sweeps' poses the pair at different elevations did. Its sigmas are the
  worst of the two-view sets. Its views are both at 20°, where gaps are 4 to
  5 px and readings are good. With ten poses, which two views are best is not
  settled.

## Not settled

- **Weighting.** Every reading counts the same, from a 5 px gap at 20° and a
  1.4 px one at 50°. The refit's `gapSigma` is there to use.
- **One render per pose.**
- **Turns over 3 degrees.** At 5 the pair's edges reach `maxAngle`.
