# The stack, off the axes and turned

The 10-02 position solve was scored on the poses its Jacobian was measured
on, every one of them moved along a single axis. Brad's two follow-ups, done
together: poses off the axes, and the top cube turned about the vertical.

## What was built

- **`gap-sweep --poses "x,y,z[,turn];..."`.** A list of poses instead of a
  sweep: a displacement from contact in mm and a turn in degrees. One shot per
  pose per view.
- **Readings at the ends.** Each pair's gap is read at its middle and a sixth
  in from each end: `endsTruePx`, `endsDetectedPx`, the refit's `endsPx`.
- **`npm run position` with `--turn`, `--test` and `--readings`.** The turn as
  a fourth unknown, from a `--poses` run of turns at the reference; test poses
  scored separately from the poses the Jacobian came from.

```bash
npm run gap-sweep -- --name stack-2g-turn --scene saved:stack-2 --moving Cube2 --target Cube \
    --poses "0,4,0,-3;0,4,0,-1.5;0,4,0,0;0,4,0,1.5;0,4,0,3" \
    --yaw 35,60 --elevation 20,50 --script pipelines/pairs.lab
npm run gap-sweep -- --name stack-2g-test --scene saved:stack-2 --moving Cube2 --target Cube \
    --poses "1.5,5,-1,0;-2,3,2,0;0.7,6,1.3,0;-1.2,4.5,-2.5,0;2.5,2.5,0.8,0;1,4,-1,1.5;-1.5,5,1.5,-2;0.5,3,-0.5,2.5;-0.8,6,2.2,-1;2,4.5,0,-3" \
    --yaw 35,60 --elevation 20,50 --script pipelines/pairs.lab
npm run position -- --x results/stack-2g-x/pairs --y results/stack-2g-y/pairs --z results/stack-2g-z/pairs \
    --turn results/stack-2g-turn/pairs --test results/stack-2g-test/pairs --max-views 4
```

60 renders. The test poses' y is 2.5 to 6 mm (the reference is a 4 mm lift);
x and z within ±2.5 mm; five turned by 1 to 3 degrees.

## What the turn needed

**Its Jacobian at the middle of a pair is nearly nothing**, 0.03 to 0.11 px per
degree, and at the ends it is large and opposite: end 1 −0.31 to −0.78, end 2
+0.30 to +0.80 px/deg. Solved from middles only, with the turn as an unknown,
every pose was refused by `--max-sigma`: the turn's sigma was 14 to 45 degrees
per pixel.

**One view with the ends determines the turn and not the rest**: 0.75 to 1.6
degrees per px, and 500 to 2100 mm per px on x, y and z. Six readings, four
unknowns, and they still do not separate the three displacements.

## Worth noting

- **The refit's end readings are worse than its middles.** At the reference
  pose, front pair, the truth's ends differ by +0.21 px; the refit's by −0.13.
  A line fitted over 100 px places its middle well and its slope less well.
  The end readings still carry the turn, but this is the place to improve.
- **The same-elevation pair (35/20 + 60/20) did best on the test poses**, where
  on the sweeps' poses the pair at different elevations did. Its sigmas are the
  worst of the two-view sets. Its views are both at 20°, where gaps are 4 to
  5 px and readings are good. With ten poses, which two views are best is not
  settled.

## Not settled

- **Weighting.** Every reading counts the same, from a 5 px gap at 20° and a
  1.4 px one at 50°. The refit's `gapSigma` is there to use.
- **One render per pose.**
- **Turns over 3 degrees.** At 5 the pair's edges reach `maxAngle`.

## Later: the ends, and the weights

Brad: start on the two improvements -- weight readings by gapSigma, and
sharpen the refit's slopes at the ends. Measured first, and the two turned
out to be one.

**gapSigma does not track a reading's error.** About 0.02 px everywhere;
the error 0.01 to 0.2, set by which pair and view it is. Rank correlation
with |error| over the test poses: 0.34 (the fit's rms, 0.41; contrast, -0.19).

**The ends were not noise.** Per view and pair, the refit's end readings
were off by a constant: front pair +0.16 at end 1, -0.16 at end 2, middle
~0; side pair the other way. Every view, every sweep. The detections' ends
had no such tilt. Each fitted edge was turned ~0.12 deg against its truth
edge, the moving one way and the target the other: scissors.

Hypothesis 1, the strip brightening along the pair (width and level trade):
sloping the strip's level alone changed nothing. Hypothesis 2, the faces:
a slope on every level killed the tilt, 0.3 -> 0.02 px on the y sweep.
`fitPairs` v3, `levelSlope=fit` by default.

Then position. Mean RMS over the 11 multi-view sets, x/y/z mm, turn deg:

    sweep poses                         test poses
    flat, unweighted    .18 .18 .14 .18   .30 .31 .34 .25
    flat, weighted      .15 .14 .14 .17   .29 .34 .38 .18
    sloped, unweighted  .18 .18 .13 .12   .29 .30 .32 .19
    sloped, weighted    .09 .10 .08 .06   .18 .25 .30 .05

Neither alone; both together. Weighting with the ends still tilted trusts
confident wrong readings. `npm run position` weights by default now.

Not every set improved: 35/20 + 60/20, best before, went 0.27/0.10/0.14 ->
0.34/0.16/0.23; 35/20 + 60/50 got worse in z, 0.61 -> 0.72.

What is left: the side pair scatters 0.13-0.18 px pose to pose, the front
pair 0.02-0.04. Its worst readings are at its widest gaps (4.4-5.8 px),
0.35-0.41 short, with ordinary rms and sigma. The eighth's crease.

All from --skip-render reanalysis of the same renders: still one per pose.
findPairs still fits flat levels; not tried.

## And then the op

Brad: build the trackPair op and wire it into gap-sweep.

- `trackPair(G, x0=, y0=, x1=, y1=, towardX=, towardY=, stripLevel=,
  aperture=, guess=)` -> one `edge-track` record. The carried values are
  parameters: numbers in the command, so in the frame's log.
- `lab-cli --extra <json>`: per-image commands after the script. The
  language still has no variables; the driver writes the numbers in.
- `gap-sweep --carry [--carry-min 3]`: pass 1 as ever; per view and pair,
  the widest frame whose refit read it as a detected pair over 3 px; every
  narrower frame reruns with `K<pair> = trackPair(G, ...)`; analysed again.
  Tracked columns, a map per gap, carry.json and carry-commands.json.

Rerun over stack-2g-approach (--skip-render --carry): the 18 tracked
readings match the notes script's to 0.002 px. The difference is the start:
the script started from the previous frame's tracked gap, the driver starts
from the carried frame's.

Contact came out empty at first: the truth has no facing pair there (the
edges coincide), so there was no row to hang a track on. Now each track is
a row of its own at contact, numbered by its line's angle, error = its gap.
0.096, 0.050, 0.080 px, as in the experiment; the other views' fits refuse.
