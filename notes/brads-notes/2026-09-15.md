# 2026-09-15

# Two checks before an `explain` v3

09-12 ended with the 88 taken apart and a question left in the conversation
rather than on disk: **is a v3 warranted?** The answer given was yes, but not
the rule 09-12's note sketched. Asking for the residual at two offsets drops 5
of the 199 real steps. What worked on helmet-256 was **the jump fit itself**:
call occlusion only where the depth pass has a real break of ≥ 2 cm (09-12,
`05-jump.js fixed`). It kept all 199 and removed all 37 curvature misreads.

Two things had to be checked before building it:

1. **What the jump fit takes away that might be real.** It reclassifies 68
   *matched* v2 occlusions on helmet-256. 49 pair with crease truth and grow
   linearly with the offset, a fold read as a step, which is the expected
   case. The other 19 pair with silhouette or boundary truth, which should
   carry a step.
2. **Whether it holds anywhere but helmet-256.** helmet-512 and clutter.

Scripts, sheets and an index are in
[`2026-09-15-v3-checks/`](2026-09-15-v3-checks/README.md).

## The answer, first

**The jump fit holds up on both checks.** It loses no real step under a
detection on any set. The two it "loses" on helmet-512 are at the growth test's
2 cm floor and look like folds, as the truth edge they matched says they are.

**But checking it surfaced the question that actually decides v3.** Should
`explain` attribute a line to an occluding contour 2-2.5 px *beside* it? The
jump fit does not settle that, and the obvious fix does not either.

## The loader holds on sets it was never run on

09-12's loader reproduces every stored `explain` record on all three: 983,
2,965 and 203 records, zero cause mismatches, zero difference in
`depthExcess`. All three are `explain` v2 with identical parameters. helmet-512
and clutter are 512 px; clutter's `maxDepth` is 4.33 m against the helmet's
10.04.

## Check 2: the rules on all three sets

Two reference populations, neither defined by the jump fit:

- **real steps**: 09-10's growth test. Raw depth difference at offset 8 under
  twice that at offset 1, and ≥ 2 cm at offset 1. A step does not grow with
  where you look.
- **scaling**: v2 occlusions whose residual grows super-linearly at *both*
  offset steps (exponent ≥ 1.5 for 1.25→2.5 and for 2.5→5). A surface, a fold
  or a neighbouring contour, not a step under the line. R1 and R3 are built
  from this scaling, so their rows against it are partly circular; J's are
  not.

Rules: **v2** as it is · **R1** also ≥ 2 cm at offset 1.25 · **R3** exponent
< 1.5 or ≥ 5 cm at 1.25 · **J** jump ≥ 2 cm · **J+loc** J with the break
within 1.5 px of the line.

```
helmet-256              v2    R1    R3     J  J+loc
real steps, matched    199   194   194   199   199
real steps, invented     9     8     8     9     9      (of 13)
scaling, matched        38    10     2    35     2
scaling, invented       26     0     0     8     0
all matched occl.      453   341   361   385   306
all invented occl.      88    34    41    51    32

helmet-512              v2    R1    R3     J  J+loc
real steps, matched    400   395   397   398   397      (of 401)
real steps, invented    21    21    21    21    21      (of 29)
scaling, matched        40     3     0    28     1
scaling, invented       27     0     0    13     2
all matched occl.      829   648   681   695   592
all invented occl.     116    47    66    76    44

clutter                 v2    R1    R3     J  J+loc
real steps, matched     52    52    52    52    52
all matched occl.       61    56    61    58    56
all invented occl.       1     0     0     1     0
```

- **Clutter has nothing to decide.** One invented occlusion and no scaling
  cases. It says only that no rule breaks a scene whose steps are real
  silhouettes against a background.
- **J is the only rule that keeps every real step on helmet-256, and it loses 2
  of 400 on helmet-512.** R1 loses 5 on each.
- **J leaves "scaling" occlusions behind: 8 and 13 invented.** Those are steps
  *beside* the line (next section). J+loc removes them, but at a price no
  population here can price: it drops 79 more *matched* occlusions than J at
  256 and 103 more at 512, and the growth test certifies none of them either
  way. A silhouette on a slanted surface grows with the offset *and* is a real
  step, which is exactly what 09-10 found the growth test cannot see.

### The two real steps J loses at 512

p0-l0 #21 and p0-l1 #41 (`sheets/helmet-512-lost-real-steps-0.png`). Both
matched **crease** truth. Both read exactly 2.0 cm at offset 1, the growth
test's floor, and barely more at 8. In the crops they run along the edge of a
smooth band on the crown: the normal pass changes, the depth-step map shows
nothing. Folds, which is what the truth says. **The growth test is what is
wrong about these two, not J.**

(helmet-512 also has one real step v2 itself does not call occlusion, p0-l1
#348, at 1.8 cm. It is a crease too.)

## Check 1: what J takes from silhouette and boundary matches

`03-crops.js` draws every matched v2 occlusion that J reclassifies whose truth
edge is a silhouette or boundary: 19 at 256, 48 at 512. **None is a real step
by the growth test**: the depth difference grows 5-22× from offset 1 to 8 on
all 19.

By eye, all 19 at 256 px:

| reading | n | detections |
|---|---|---|
| beside a contour 2-2.5 px away | 10 | the gold panel's frame (p1-l0 #87, p1-l1 #94, p2-l0 #89 #90, p2-l1 #101); a panel's lower edge (p1-l0 #40, p1-l1 #46, p2-l1 #46); a narrow ridge (p2-l0 #64, p2-l1 #67) |
| along the top vent slot, its lip further off than the fit reaches | 3 | p1-l0 #11, p2-l0 #11, p2-l1 #11 |
| visor rim, no break at all | 6 | p0-l0 #57 #60 #77, p0-l1 #66 #71 #89 |

The visor rim is a **boundary** edge, the model's opening, with the pane
sitting flush behind it. A boundary does not have to be a step, and here it is
not one. These are long (17-39 px) and match their truth edge to within 1 px
and 8°, so they are J being right about something v2 got wrong. One oddity is
recorded and not explained: their residual is ~3 cm and *flat* from offset 1.25
to 2.5, with no break in the depth. A flat residual with no break is what a
tangent plane built from the wrong normal would give. **If the normal pass
carries a normal map, the plane is built on shading normals**, not the
geometry's. Unverified.

At 512 px I looked at 24 of the 48 (sheets 0, 2, 4, 6), not all. They show the
same places at twice the resolution: beside the gold pin's silhouette, the
visor rim again, lines on a face next to a narrow slot, a few at junctions. Two
or three sit along a narrow bevel whose hidden face is under a pixel wide.
Between a fold and a step at that width, the depth pass cannot say which.
**Nothing among the 24 shows a break under the line that J missed.** By
position of the fit's break, the 48 are 25 with no break within reach, 17 with
a sub-2 cm break within 1.5 px, and 6 with a break beside.

## What this leaves for v3

- **J, a real break in the depth, is supported as the occlusion test.** Across
  three sets it keeps every real step the growth test can vouch for, except two
  that are folds the growth test mis-certified. It removes the curvature and
  fold misreads, including the flush visor rim.
- **Steps beside the line are the open design question, and they are not
  rare.** On the matched side, 10 of the 19 here are a detected line
  2-2.5 px from a contour that `match` pairs it with. The 79 (256) and 103
  (512) matched occlusions J keeps and J+loc drops are probably the same
  thing, but none of them has been looked at. Whether that line was "put there by the
  occlusion" is a definition, not a measurement: the intensity edge is often
  the rim's bevel or shading beside the contour, not the contour. Deciding it
  would take a population certified some other way, perhaps by distance to the
  truth contour at a higher render resolution than the one detected at.
- **The numbers v3 would publish**, if J alone: invented occlusion 88 → 51 at
  256 and 116 → 76 at 512. Matched occlusion 453 → 385 and 829 → 695, most of
  them moving to crease.
- **Not examined:** at 512, 68 of the 116 invented v2 occlusions fall in
  neither reference population, so nothing here says what they are. J keeps
  some of them, and no one has looked.

## Caveats

- Two subjects, 256 and 512 px, one detector configuration, `explain`'s default
  thresholds, J's 2 cm threshold carried over from 09-12 without retuning.
- The jump fit's window is in pixels and its threshold in metres. It held at
  512, which halves the metres per pixel; a coarser render has not been tried.
- The growth test has a floor at its own 2 cm, and both of J's "losses" sit on
  it.
