## The model

fitBand: a soft edge `bound` (cannot pass the still edge: the ledge ends
there) and `within` (cannot pass the moving edge), clamped like the
occluder; the lit level, the width and the position may then have no pixel
and are held, not refused. Anchored with an `offset` from the moving edge:
the shadow moves with it. trackPair: `ledge: 'fit'` (free) or
`{ offset, width }` (held). Hidden behind the still edge it is the plain
model exactly (tested to 1e-9).

retrack.js re-runs a --carry run's trackPair commands in node (G from the
PNG as notes/2026-10-04-carry/lib does; reproduces the lab's readings to the
third decimal) and scores them with gapRows.
